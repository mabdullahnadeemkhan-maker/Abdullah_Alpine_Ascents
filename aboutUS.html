A Single Hand-Milled Cedar Cabin in the Swiss Bernese Oberland

Founded by architect Elena Lindqvist and mountain guide Lukas Meyer, Alpine Ascents began by restoring an abandoned 19th-century timber herder’s refuge using salvaged larch wood, triple-glazed passive glass, and zero concrete foundations.
